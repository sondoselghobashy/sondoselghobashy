About

I'm a software engineer based in Berlin with more than seven years of experience building backend systems, REST APIs and the infrastructure behind them, now completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Universität zu Lübeck.

My work sits where engineering meets research: I train and evaluate deep neural networks in PyTorch, and I focus on the security and trustworthiness of ML systems — defending models against adversarial manipulation, data poisoning and backdoor attacks — alongside AI governance and the EU AI Act. I'm particularly drawn to medical and safety-critical applications, where a model's reliability matters as much as its accuracy.

Most recently I was a software engineer at the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI), building the platforms that research runs on: authentication and access control, data pipelines, admin tooling and an experience-based reasoning agent.
